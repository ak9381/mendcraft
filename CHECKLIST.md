# Mendcraft checklist (deadline Dec 1, 3:30 AM IST)

## Hardware checks
- [ ] Gimbal: dock the iPhone in the Insta360 Flow 2 Pro and check that it tracks you.
- [ ] WiZ lamp: pair it in the app and do one test of the light changing.
- [ ] Chime pairing: Nothing 3a on the left, iPhone on the right, and check that they pair.
- [ ] iPhone app: open Mendcraft Relay, check the canvas shows, then tap the mic and look for the "Apple · on device" badge.
- [ ] Phone sync: two-fist grab a board on the laptop, drag it to the iPhone, then look at the phone to unlock touch.

## Live tests on the Mac
- [ ] Hands: 1.5 s hover-select, two-fist move, and no glitching near buttons, with the left and right hand both.
- [ ] Voice: say "hi how are you" and check you get a real answer from Nano or Gemini.
- [ ] Gemini real run: one on-device answer and one cloud answer in Chrome, not the simulation.

## Build
- [ ] Move the iPhone screen changes (wip/iphone-canvas 177964a) onto sim-main.
- [ ] Android build on the Nothing 3a (USB debugging needs to be on).
- [ ] Replay mode so judges can try it without a camera (see HANDOVER.md §14).

## Demo and submission
- [ ] Review the demo script (DEMO.md, coming next).
- [ ] Record the video, about 3 minutes, showing both on-device and cloud.
- [ ] Upload it to YouTube early, since processing is slow.
- [ ] Review the Devpost draft together: the try-it link, the video link and visibility. Only then submit.
