Which model answers, and why

Gemini Nano runs in Chrome on your computer for quick, private work. Gemini in the cloud, reached through Firebase AI Logic, takes the jobs that need more. A router picks one for every request and shows you why. Then a Mendcraft app carries out the command.

Buttons stay large enough to pinch.

Most targets are 56 px, and you can make every one of them 72. That is the first of our accessibility promises. The rest hold on every screen too.

  • No grip, drag or double-clickEvery action works with a pinch, a dwell or your voice.
  • Your timing, not oursSet dwell anywhere from 0.3 to 2 seconds. Nothing times out while you think.
  • Captions on every wordLive captions in your language with the English beside it, large and high contrast.
  • Your camera stays yoursVideo and hand points are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Undo anything, out loudSay “undo” or “ರದ್ದುಮಾಡು” at any time, as often as you need.
  • Clear contrast everywhereEvery screen meets WCAG 2.2 AA, and body text meets AAA.
